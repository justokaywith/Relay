# Community code of conduct

Relay welcomes people with different backgrounds, skills and ways of working. You can contribute without being an AI engineer.

## Be a useful collaborator

- Treat people respectfully and discuss ideas on their merits.
- Give actionable feedback with examples. Assume good intent and welcome questions.
- Respect privacy. Never share credentials, private conversations or another person's personal information.
- Keep discussions relevant. Disclose affiliations when recommending a product or service.
- Make room for others; avoid repeated unsolicited messages.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, impersonation, doxxing, spam and deliberately harmful instructions are not welcome.

## Reporting and moderation

For conduct concerns, email **support@pactuno.online** with a link and a concise description. Share only the information needed to investigate. Maintainers will handle reports with discretion; do not post sensitive evidence publicly.

For software vulnerabilities, use [private security reporting](https://github.com/justokaywith/Relay/security/advisories/new) instead.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, ask someone to change their behaviour, restrict participation, or block repeated or serious abuse. Decisions should be proportionate to the behaviour and protect the community.

This policy applies to this repository, its issues, pull requests and Discussions. Maintainers are expected to follow it too.
